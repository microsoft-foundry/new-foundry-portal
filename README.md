# The Azure AI Foundry Portal - Private Preview Feedback Hub

Welcome to the feedback space for the **The Aure AI Foundry Portal Private Preview**. This repository is your direct line to the Azure AI Foundry Product Group as we shape the next generation of developer experience together.

## Purpose

This repo is designed to:

- Collect **bugs**, **suggestions**, and **praise** from preview participants, and triage them effectively and promptly.
- Share **updates** and **developments** from the Product Group.

## How to Contribute

We encourage you to:

- **Submit issues** using GitHub’s issue tracker.
- Use **tags** to categorize your feedback (e.g., `bug`, `suggestion`, `praise`, `question`).
- Use **`[issue]` tags** to provide details your feeedback when applicable.
- Use **`[feature]` tags** to indicate which feature your feedback relates to.
- **Monitor your issues** after submission — we may follow up with questions or clarifications.

### What to Include

When submitting feedback, please include:

- A clear description of the issue or suggestion.
- Steps to reproduce (if applicable).
- Screenshots or logs (if possible).
- Your thoughts on how it could be improved or why it works well.

## Stay Updated

We’ll use this space to:

- Announce new features and improvements.
- Share known issues and workarounds.
- Provide timelines and progress updates.
